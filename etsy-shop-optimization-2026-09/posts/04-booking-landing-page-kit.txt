This started as a request in a freelance job post, so I built it properly: a landing page kit with three starting points (barber/salon, consultant/coach, local appointment service), all sharing one render engine, plus an in-browser customiser — fill in your business name, colours, services, hours and FAQs, watch it update live, and download one finished index.html. Nothing uploaded anywhere; it runs entirely in your own browser.
