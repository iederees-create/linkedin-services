# Custom job and inventory systems for repair businesses

I build custom job and inventory systems for independent appliance repair technicians and small repair businesses. The demonstrated workflow is intentionally focused: create a job, record diagnosis, add labour and parts, issue stock explicitly, update status, then produce an estimate or invoice.

This is a service enquiry, not a preconfigured software subscription. Scope, platform, data fields, validation rules and delivery dates are agreed after a short conversation. The public demo uses fictional data and has no login or database. The DIY spreadsheet kit is a separate product.
