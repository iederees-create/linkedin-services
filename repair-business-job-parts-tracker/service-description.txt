I build custom job and inventory systems for independent appliance repair technicians and small repair businesses.

We can map your workflow from job intake to diagnosis, parts and labour, status updates, estimates, invoices and stock movements. The demonstration keeps stock changes explicit: parts are issued when used, returns are recorded deliberately, and issued invoice prices remain snapshots when catalogue prices change.

Typical scope includes an agreed Excel or web-based workflow, search and filters, validation, printable documents, import/export and a handover guide. Complex integrations, payments, SMS, route planning, accounting connections and multi-user permissions require separately agreed scope.
