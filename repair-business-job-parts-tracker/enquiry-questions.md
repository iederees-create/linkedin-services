# Enquiry questions

1. What type of repairs do you handle and who will use the system?
2. What must happen from first customer contact to completed invoice?
3. Which tabs, fields, statuses and calculations do you already use?
4. Can you provide one anonymised sample job and the expected estimate/invoice output?
5. Do you prefer Excel/LibreOffice or a browser application?
6. Which branding, currency, tax display and document wording should be configurable?
7. What is the deadline, priority and smallest useful first release?
8. Do you need import/export, backups, approvals or integrations in a later phase?
