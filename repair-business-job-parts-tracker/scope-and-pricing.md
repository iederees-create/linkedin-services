# Scope and pricing

Pricing is proposed after discovery, not a fixed platform package. A small first version can cover a job register, parts catalogue, explicit movement ledger, line items and printable estimate/invoice. Larger work may include a hosted database, accounts, audit history or integrations only after requirements and privacy responsibilities are agreed.

Excluded from the demonstrated first version: payment processing, route optimisation, SMS, accounting integrations, live supplier feeds, multi-user collaboration and tax compliance advice.
