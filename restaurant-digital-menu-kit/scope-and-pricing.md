# Scope and pricing

Pricing is proposed after a short discovery conversation, not a fixed package price. A first version typically covers: applying your branding (logo, colours, contact details) to one of the three included themes, entering your menu content, and setting up the TV, mobile and printable views with your content.

**Proposed starting scope, illustrative only:** branding + content setup on the existing kit, one round of revisions, and a short walkthrough of how to update the menu yourself afterwards. Final price depends on menu size, how much custom design work is wanted beyond the existing three themes, and hosting/setup assistance requested.

**Explicitly excluded unless separately scoped and agreed:** hardware/screen purchase or installation, ongoing subscription-style support, online ordering or payment integration, and native digital-signage platform integrations.

This is a proposed starting point, not a binding quote, and does not commit to ongoing support or a subscription.
