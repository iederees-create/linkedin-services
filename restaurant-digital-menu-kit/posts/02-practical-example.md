Small example of why I built the digital menu kit this way: a café owner sells out of something, or changes a price, or runs a Tuesday special. That update needs to show up consistently on the TV, on the phone menu people scan at the table, and on the printed copy at the counter — not just one of them.

With this kit, you make the change once in the editor, save, and every view (TV, mobile, print) reads from the same saved menu. No separate systems to keep in sync by hand.
