I just finished building something I'd wanted for a while: a digital menu kit for cafés, takeaways and food trucks.

The idea is simple — enter your menu once, and get a TV display, a mobile-friendly page, and a printable menu, all from the same content. No coding, no subscription, no developer needed for the DIY version.

It handles the small things that actually matter day to day: sold-out items, promo slides, three different themes depending on your kind of business, and long menus that paginate cleanly on a TV screen instead of shrinking the text until nobody can read it.

I've open a DIY version as a digital download, and I'm also taking on a small number of branded setups where I do it for you — your logo, your colours, your menu, ready to go.

Happy to talk through it if you run a café, takeaway or food truck and want to see it. Link to the working demo in the comments/first comment.
