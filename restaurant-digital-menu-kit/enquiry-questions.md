# Enquiry questions

Ask a prospective client:

1. What type of business is this for (café, takeaway, food truck, small restaurant)?
2. Roughly how many menu categories and items do you have?
3. Do you already have a logo and brand colours?
4. Do you have a screen/TV in mind for the display, and does it already have a browser or connected device?
5. Do you have web hosting already, or would you need help choosing a simple option?
6. Do you want printed menus as well, and in what size (A4 / US Letter)?
7. Any dietary/allergen labelling you need reflected (you'll need to confirm the exact wording yourself)?
8. Any specific timeline?
